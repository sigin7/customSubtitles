Custom Subtitles

This installer includes Node.js 24 LTS and the official Tailscale Windows installer.
Node.js is installed privately with this application and does not change your system PATH.
Tailscale is installed only if missing and you select its installation task.

Tailscale installation may require a Windows administrator prompt. Signing into
your own Tailscale account and enabling Funnel are separate first-run steps.
Tailscale is required on the server only; playback devices do not need it.
